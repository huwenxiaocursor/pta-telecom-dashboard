    结论What this shows

      线路与上网出口Line and internet egress

      响应时间（由近及远）Response time, nearest to farthest

      每个点连续请求 20 次。网页测的是一次完整的 HTTPS 请求，数值会比 ping 高一些；判断时主要看抖动、卡顿次数和失败，不要只看平均值。"卡顿次数"是比平时慢一倍以上的请求数，通常是丢包后重传造成的 —— 网页测不了丢包率，这是最接近的指标。 20 requests per point. A browser measures a full HTTPS request, so values run higher than ping; judge mainly by jitter, spikes and failures, not the average. "Spikes" counts requests at least twice as slow as usual - usually a lost packet being retransmitted, the closest a browser gets to measuring loss.

      带宽与满载响应Bandwidth and responsiveness under load

      "满载时延增加"指下载或上传跑满时，响应时间比空闲时多了多少。带宽够、但这个数很大，说明线路一忙就排队（bufferbloat），语音视频会卡，加带宽解决不了。 "Added latency under load" is how much response time grows while the line is saturated. Good bandwidth with a large increase means queues bloat under load (bufferbloat): voice and video suffer, and more bandwidth will not fix it.

      开口前先确认三件事Three things to check first

      1

      T2 能检验客户有没有测对地方T2 tells you whether the customer tested correctly

      客户真的接在报障的那条专线上，T2 应该是个位数毫秒。如果 T2 跑出几百毫秒，说明他根本不在那条线上 —— 可能用的是手机热点、家里宽带或另一个办公室。On a machine actually on the reported circuit, T2 is single-digit milliseconds. If it comes back in the hundreds, the customer ran the tool somewhere else - phone hotspot, home broadband, another office.

      这种情况整份报告作废，让客户在专线上重测。不用问客户任何问题，这一档自己会暴露。The whole report is void; ask them to re-run on the circuit. No questions needed - this tier gives it away.

      2

      用 T2b 之前，先确认它会老实回应 ICMPBefore trusting T2b, confirm it answers ICMP reliably

      实测中 223.123.28.17 连续三次丢包 8%、12%、36%，而同一路径上的 103.150.184.61 三次都是 0%。只有一个地址剧烈抖动，是 ICMP 限速的典型特征，不是真故障。Off-net testing gave 223.123.28.17 8%, 12% and 36% loss on three runs while 103.150.184.61 on the same path stayed at 0%. Loss swinging on one address alone is the signature of ICMP rate limiting, not a fault.

      在巴基斯坦当地一条正常专线上验证：Verify from a healthy circuit in Pakistan:
      →不丢包：这一档可以直接用Clean: usable as-is →仍然丢包：换一个探测地址，路由器环回口通常更老实Still lossy: replace the probe; a router loopback answers ICMP more honestly →找不到合适地址：去掉这一档，T2 已覆盖 Zong 网内第一跳No good address: drop the tier; T2 already covers the first Zong hop

      不处理的后果：每份报告都带一个吓人的假警报，支持部门看几次就会习惯性忽略整个 T2b 档，这一档还不如不加。Left alone, every report carries a scary false alarm, and support soon learns to ignore T2b altogether.

      3

      T3 是国际参照，不是"本地"T3 is international, not "local"

      8.8.8.8 是 Google 的任播 DNS：同一个地址在全球几十个国家都有节点，你连上的是最近的那个。它反映的是国际出口和互联质量。8.8.8.8 is Google anycast DNS: the same address is served from many countries and you reach the nearest node. Its latency reflects international egress and peering.

      要和 T4（国内方向）对照着看，不能当成"本地基准"。快慢取决于 Google 在当地有没有节点、互联好不好。Read it against T4 (China direction), never as a local baseline.

      判读前必看的五个陷阱Five traps - read before you judge

      1中间跳的丢包通常是假的Intermediate-hop loss is usually fake

      路由器普遍对发给自己的 ICMP 限速，但转发流量完全正常。只有最后一跳（目标）的丢包才是真的。真正的故障表现为：从第 N 跳开始丢，且后面每一跳都持续丢。中间某一跳丢 100%、后面几跳却全部正常 —— 那只是那台设备不回应 ping。Routers rate-limit ICMP replies to themselves while forwarding traffic perfectly. Only loss at the FINAL hop is real. A genuine fault shows loss starting at hop N and persisting on every hop after it.

      Windows 用 pathping，macOS 用 traceroute 加并行 ping。工具不同，判读方法完全一样。Windows uses pathping; macOS runs traceroute then pings every hop in parallel. Different tools, identical interpretation.

      2ICMP 和 TCP 待遇不同ICMP and TCP are treated differently

      有些节点对 ICMP 限速甚至丢弃，但 TCP 完全正常，反过来也有。第 4 节是 TCP 层的证据。客户的业务走的是 TCP，判断时应该以它为准，不要只看 ping。Some nodes deprioritise or drop ICMP while TCP is fine, and the reverse also happens. Section 4 is the TCP-layer evidence. Business traffic is TCP - weight it accordingly.

      3首字节慢但建连快 = 对端服务器慢High TTFB with low TCP/TLS means the server is slow

      第 4 节把一次访问拆成：DNS 解析 / TCP 建连 / TLS 握手 / 首字节返回。如果网络段只有 40 毫秒，首字节却要 3 秒，那是对端应用的问题，不是网络问题。这一条能澄清相当一部分"线路很慢"的工单。Section 4 splits the delay into DNS / TCP / TLS / first byte. Network at 40 ms with first byte at 3 s is a far-end application problem, not a transport problem.

      在高时延链路上，光 TLS 握手就可能是建连耗时的三倍，因为它要来回好几趟。这是物理距离决定的，不是故障 —— 解法是 HTTP/2、连接复用或加速服务，而不是开故障单。On a high-latency path the TLS handshake alone can be three times the TCP connect time, because it needs several round trips. That is physics, not a fault - the answer is HTTP/2, connection reuse or an acceleration service, not a repair ticket.

      4小包通、大包丢 = MTU 问题Large packets lost while small pass = MTU

      第 5 节从 1472 字节往下递减试探。专线上叠了 VPN 或 GRE 隧道时最常见。客户的描述通常是"能 ping 通，但网页打不开、文件传一半就断"。Section 5 probes 1472 down to 1272. Typical on circuits carrying VPN or GRE tunnels. The customer says "ping works but pages hang and file transfers stall".

      几乎没人会往 MTU 上想，所以这类单子经常拖很久，而它只需要调一下 CPE 的 MTU 或开 MSS clamping。Almost nobody thinks of MTU, so these tickets drag on - yet the fix is a CPE MTU change or MSS clamping.

      抖动Jitter> 30 ms 异常。平均时延看着正常但客户抱怨卡，多半是抖动造成的> 30 ms abnormal, and the usual cause of "slow" complaints even when average latency looks fine
      时延Latency要和报告里的基准值比，不要看绝对值。卡拉奇到中国超过 100 毫秒是正常的物理距离Judge against the baseline in the report, not an absolute number - Karachi to China is legitimately over 100 ms

      抖动和丢包比平均时延更能解释"卡"。语音和视频在抖动上先垮掉，那时候时延还远没到成为问题的程度。Jitter and loss explain perceived slowness far better than average latency. Voice and video degrade on jitter long before latency becomes the issue.

      持续监测的摘要是对时间的钥匙The monitor summary is the correlation key

      监测摘要里"出现丢包或时延升高的分钟"那一段，给的是带时间戳的具体窗口。拿这些分钟去比对网管侧的接口计数、告警和流量图。The "MINUTES WITH LOSS OR ELEVATED LATENCY" block gives timestamped windows. Correlate those exact minutes against interface counters, alarms and utilisation graphs.

      这把"客户说今天下午很卡"变成了一个你真能去查的具体时间窗；同时，当故障不在我们这边时，它也是保护我们的证据。It turns "the customer says this afternoon was slow" into a window you can actually check - and protects us when the fault is not ours.

      随附的 .csv 一行一个探测点。用 Excel 打开，以时间为横轴、rtt_ms 为纵轴画曲线，给客户看图比讲道理有用。The accompanying .csv is one row per probe. Open it in Excel and chart rtt_ms against timestamp to show the customer what happened.

      macOS 版的三点差异macOS version - what is different

      档位结构和判读陷阱完全相同，只有三点不一样。Same six-tier structure and same traps; three differences.

      1. 第 4b 节的 bufferbloat 检测只有 macOS 版有1. Section [4b] bufferbloat is macOS only

      Apple 的 networkQuality 给出 RPM，即线路满载时的响应性。客户可能带宽完全达标、测速也漂亮，但就是一直觉得卡 —— 因为一满载队列就膨胀：语音断续、视频卡顿、网页停顿。Apple's networkQuality reports RPM - responsiveness under load. A customer can have full bandwidth and still feel constant slowness because queues bloat under load: voice breaks up, video freezes, pages stall.

      RPM < 300明显卡顿poor, feels laggy
      RPM 300–1000可接受acceptable
      RPM > 1000良好good

      带宽够但 RPM 低，是队列问题不是容量问题，给客户扩带宽完全没用。Low RPM with good throughput is a queueing problem, not a capacity problem. More bandwidth will not fix it.

      2. 逐跳丢包的实现方式不同2. Per-hop loss is measured differently

      macOS 没有 pathping，脚本改用 traceroute 拿到跳表后对每一跳并行打 20 个包。比 pathping 快很多，判读方法完全一样，中间跳的 ICMP 限速陷阱同样适用。macOS has no pathping, so the script runs traceroute and then pings every hop in parallel, 20 probes each. Faster, identical interpretation - and the ICMP rate-limit trap applies just as much.

      3. 抖动值是 ping 原生给出的3. Jitter comes straight from ping

      macOS 的 ping 直接输出标准差，所以抖动是样本的真实标准差。只收到一个回包时无法计算，报告会显示 n/a。macOS ping reports stddev natively, so jitter is the real standard deviation of the sample. With only one reply it prints n/a.
